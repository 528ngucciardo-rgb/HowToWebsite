Steps

Step 1:

Take out all your ingredients

Step 2:

Put your pan on the stove and turn the stove on to medium heat

Step 3:

Grab the two pieces of bread

Step 4:

Smear butter on the outside of each slice of bread

Step 5:

Place one slice of bread on the heated pan

Step 6:

Start building the sandwich, add the cheese and the other piece of bread

Step 7:

Wait 2 minutes and check the underside with a spatula for golden brown

Step 8:

Flip the bread and grill the other side

Step 9:

Remove the grilled cheese from the grill

Step 10:

Enjoy!
